# BOM notes

Prices are indicative concept estimates (TRL 2) and will be confirmed with named suppliers at TRL 3. Item numbers match the exploded view (`media/exploded.png`) and the component table in FCL-PRC-001; item 17 (wiring and hardware) is not modeled.

Parts total about $1,462, within the $1,500 concept budget in `project.yaml` but with only about $38 (2.5 %) of margin and no allowance for tools, spares, ground stakes beyond the basic set, or shipping. The battery pack (item 6), PV panels (item 13) and inverter (item 9) are about half the total, so their prices decide whether the budget holds. Any budget change is proposed in `docs/REVIEW.md` and awaits Amish.

Make items are priced at material cost only.
